# Privacy Policy for TvApp

**Effective Date:** May 24, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how **TvApp** ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe experience.

### 1. Information We Collect
We do not collect any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address. The app is designed to function without requiring a user account or registration.

### 2. Technical Data
To provide a smooth streaming experience, the app may access:
*   **Device Information:** Model, OS version, and screen resolution (to optimize video playback).
*   **Network Status:** To check if you are connected to the internet.
*   **Local Storage:** The app saves your channel list (M3U file) locally on your device to allow you to customize your experience.

### 3. Third-Party Services
Our app uses third-party libraries and services that may collect information used to identify you:
*   **Google Play Services:** Used for app stability and performance monitoring.
*   **Media3 / ExoPlayer:** Used for video rendering and playback.
*   **GitHub:** Used only to fetch public updates for the default channel list.

### 4. Content Disclaimer (Crucial)
*   **TvApp** is a media player. We **do not** host, provide, or own any media content or IPTV channels.
*   Users are responsible for the content they add or watch through the app. 
*   We do not endorse the streaming of copyright-protected material without permission from the copyright holder.

### 5. Permissions
The app requires the following permissions to function:
*   **INTERNET:** To stream video content and fetch list updates.
*   **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** To detect connectivity issues.
*   **WAKE_LOCK:** To prevent the screen from turning off during video playback.

### 6. Security
We value your trust in using our app. Since we do not collect personal data, your privacy is maintained by default. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

### 7. Children’s Privacy
Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
